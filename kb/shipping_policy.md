# Shipping Policy

## Shipping Destinations
We ship to EU, UK, US, and Canada. Shipping to other regions is available on request.

## Shipping Costs
- EU: Free shipping over 50 EUR, otherwise 5.99 EUR
- UK: Free shipping over 60 GBP, otherwise 7.99 GBP
- US: Free shipping over 75 USD, otherwise 9.99 USD
- Canada: Flat rate 12.99 CAD

## Delivery Times
- EU: 3-5 business days
- UK: 4-6 business days
- US: 5-8 business days
- Canada: 6-10 business days

## Express Shipping
Express shipping available for additional 15 EUR / 15 USD / 12 GBP. Delivery in 1-2 business days for EU, 2-3 for others.

## Tracking
All orders include tracking number sent via email after shipment.

## Customs
For non-EU shipments, customs duties may apply and are responsibility of customer.
